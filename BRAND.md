# Fairway Flow brand

The approved site logo is the three walking golfers carrying bags above two sweeping sage fairway strokes forming an F, with the dark green italic FAIRWAY FLOW wordmark.

Use `dist/assets/brand/fairway-flow-logo.png` in future site work. This is the selected “In the Flow” direction. The concept-board caption “03 IN THE FLOW” is not part of the production logo.

Keep the complete composition, aspect ratio, green colors and wordmark. Do not replace it with a flag glyph, a different walking golfer, or another F concept. Allow clear space. Do not add a white or cream panel behind the logo. Keep the original forest-green and sage colors on light dashboard surfaces. Over dark footage, use the transparent reversed ivory treatment defined in flight.css, with a subtle shadow for contrast. The PNG asset has a transparent background and is shared by the dashboard and immersive course view.
