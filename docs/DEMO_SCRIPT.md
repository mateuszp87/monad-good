# Judge demo script

1. Open the demo on a phone. Explain: “Fairway Flow is golf traffic guidance: understand the group ahead, keep reasonable pace, and help staff find bottlenecks.”
2. Choose one of four courses. Watch without joining, then join the 08:30 demo group with FLOW0830. State that all other group positions are simulated.
3. Pan/zoom and tap a dot. Show tee time, hole, phase and pace. Return to your group. Show whole-course view to look ahead.
4. Choose Round settings → Show hole 5 delay. Tap View affected hole. Explain target, elapsed time and grace. Explicitly call the ball-search reason a scripted course note, not a GPS finding.
5. Show that the reference group is not blamed for waiting. Demonstrate same-hole yardage when available; don't manufacture a number when groups are on different holes.
6. Open operations.html. Inspect the delayed group, edit a hole target, and save a local observation. Explain that staff sessions and observations are not yet shared.
7. If asked about Monad, open dashboard.html?view=rewards. Wallet connection and balance reading are implemented; claims, tokens, credits and funded rewards are not.
8. Close with the pilot plan: course-validated targets, consented shared GPS, measured reliability and calibrated pace guidance.

## Before presenting

- Verify the live URL can be opened by a judge outside the editing account. Current Sites permissions must be checked; don't assume a production URL is publicly accessible.
- Test on real phones and the presentation network. Do not rely only on DOM tests.
- Keep a local static copy available. Native map libraries and course geometry are local; external aerial tiles still need connectivity.
- Aerial zoom beyond source detail switches to vector geometry. Explain that a licensed high-resolution source remains future work.
- Portal entry, team confirmation, any requested video/screenshots and event-specific eligibility are not replaced by this script.
